## 3. On-screen text to fix before you render

- **Mister Miracle (43):** the card says "Born on Apokolips." He was born on New Genesis and raised on Apokolips. Change `fact` to "Raised on Apokolips, he escaped it."
- **Superman (2):** "Conceived in 1932" is shaky. Safer: "Sold to DC for just $130."
- **Spelling:** Blue Beetle's fact says "armour"; US spelling is "armor." Mister Freeze shows "-100°C"; US viewers think in °F, so drop the number.
- **The Spectre (50):** `role` is "supervillain," so the badge reads VILLAIN. "antihero" fits better.
